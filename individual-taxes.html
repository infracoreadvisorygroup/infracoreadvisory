Tax Preparation Questions

Can you prepare my taxes completely virtually?

Yes. Tax preparation can be completed remotely, including document collection, communication, return review, electronic signatures, and electronic filing when available.

I have a 1099. Does that mean I'm self-employed?

Many taxpayers receiving nonemployee compensation are treated as self-employed for tax purposes. Your specific filing requirements depend on the type of income and your individual circumstances.

Can you file my Schedule C?

Yes. We prepare individual returns that include Schedule C for sole proprietors, independent contractors, freelancers, gig workers, and other qualifying self-employed taxpayers.

Can you prepare taxes if I live outside Texas?

Virtual preparation is available, and many federal and state returns can be prepared remotely. Contact us with the states involved so we can confirm your filing needs.

What if I haven't filed in several years?

Prior-year return preparation is available. We'll start by identifying which years need attention and what documentation is available for those returns.

Can you help if I received an IRS letter?

We can review many IRS notices, explain what the notice is requesting, and help determine the appropriate next step. The exact assistance available depends on the type of notice and representation required.

Do you guarantee a certain refund?

No. Refunds and balances due are based on your actual tax situation and applicable tax law. Our focus is preparing an accurate return based on the information and documentation provided.
