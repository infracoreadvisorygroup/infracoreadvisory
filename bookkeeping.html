Bookkeeping Questions

Not sure where your business fits? Start with what your books look like today.

What if I'm several months behind?

That's exactly what catch-up bookkeeping is for. We can review the months that need to be completed, reconcile the accounts, organize transactions, and help get your records current.

Do I have to sign up for monthly bookkeeping to get a cleanup?

No. Cleanup and catch-up bookkeeping can be completed as a separate project. If you decide afterward that you want ongoing bookkeeping, we can discuss a monthly package.

Can you work with my Jobber and QuickBooks?

Yes. We can review bookkeeping information alongside systems such as Jobber, QuickBooks, bank accounts, credit cards, and payment processors to help organize your financial activity.

Will you also prepare my business taxes?

Tax preparation is available through InfraCore. Having organized bookkeeping throughout the year can make business tax preparation significantly easier.

What does the monthly price depend on?

Pricing can vary based on transaction volume, number of accounts, complexity of the books, software integrations, and additional services your business needs.

Can you also send invoices and follow up on unpaid customers?

Yes. Invoicing, accounts receivable tracking, past-due account follow-up, collections communication, and other back-office services can be added based on your business needs.
