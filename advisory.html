Profitability

Cash Flow

Why does the bank balance keep changing?

Profit and cash are not the same thing. We help look at when money comes in, when it goes out, outstanding receivables, upcoming obligations, and how those movements affect the business.

Pricing

Is your pricing actually supporting the business?

We can review labor, materials, overhead, expenses, and target margins to help you understand whether your current pricing makes financial sense.

Expenses

Tax Planning

Growth

Business Advisory Questions

What's the difference between bookkeeping and advisory?

Bookkeeping organizes and records your financial activity. Advisory takes that information further by helping you understand what the numbers mean and how they relate to decisions involving cash flow, profitability, pricing, taxes, and growth.

Is bookkeeping included in the advisory package?

Yes. The Business Advisory package includes the bookkeeping foundation needed to provide useful financial guidance, along with ongoing strategy and planning support.

How often do we review my business?

The Business Advisory package includes a monthly strategy meeting along with ongoing financial reporting and support.

Can you help me figure out whether my pricing is profitable?

Yes. Pricing and profitability review can look at your revenue, labor, materials, overhead, expenses, and financial goals to help you better understand whether your pricing supports the business.

Do you help with tax planning too?

Yes. Tax planning is included in the Business Advisory package so taxes can be considered throughout the year rather than only when the return is being prepared.

What if my books aren't current?

We can start with catch-up or cleanup bookkeeping. Reliable advisory depends on having accurate financial information, so getting the books organized may be the first step.
