Frequently Asked Questions

A few of the questions we hear from small business owners.

Can you help if my bookkeeping is already behind?

Yes. Catch-up and cleanup bookkeeping is available for businesses that need to organize prior months or correct existing bookkeeping before moving forward.

Do you provide monthly bookkeeping?

Yes. Ongoing bookkeeping can include transaction categorization, reconciliations, financial reporting, and other services based on your business needs.

Do you work with QuickBooks?

Yes. QuickBooks support is available as part of our bookkeeping, cleanup, and business accounting services.

Can you handle my bookkeeping and my taxes?

Yes. InfraCore provides both bookkeeping and tax preparation services, allowing your financial records and tax preparation to work together.

Do you work with brand-new businesses?

Yes. New businesses can receive help establishing bookkeeping processes, organizing financial records, understanding tax responsibilities, and building better financial habits from the beginning.

Do you only work with businesses in Conroe?

No. InfraCore serves businesses in Conroe, Montgomery County, the Houston area, and works virtually with clients throughout Texas.

What if I don't know exactly what service I need?

That's okay. Start by explaining what's happening in your business. We'll help determine which service or combination of services makes sense for your situation.

What's taking up too much of your time?

Maybe it's bookkeeping. Maybe you're worried about taxes. Maybe customers owe you money, your paperwork is everywhere, or you simply don't know what your numbers are telling you. Tell us what's happening and we'll help you determine the next step.
